# ADR-NNN: Title stating the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Applies from:** task (P1-xx) or phase

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, and anything in the plan or other ADRs
that bears on it.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly. Name who made the call and when if it was a choice between
options.

## Alternatives considered

Optional. Each option that was weighed, and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder, what must now always be true, and what to revisit later.

## Related

Optional. Other ADRs this one depends on or affects.
